I am **Yongxin Guan (Kevin)**, a Year 3 student in the **Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Data Science** program at [Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University (BNBU)](https://bnbu.edu.cn/). My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD and build research that connects multimodal intelligence with practical deployment in real-world systems.

My current interests lie in **AI for Science**, **world models**, **knowledge graphs**, **agent systems**, **reinforcement learning**, and **LLM enhancement across different domains**. I enjoy working on problems that combine language, vision, structured knowledge, and robust engineering.

I am currently conducting research on multimodal relation extraction with large language models, knowledge graph completion for Graph RAG, and vision-based measurement systems. I am also open to research collaboration, internships, and opportunities where I can contribute to AI product building and applied machine learning.

My [CV](files/CV_Yongxin_GUAN.pdf) is available for download. You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/KevinKwank) and [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/yongxinkwan).
